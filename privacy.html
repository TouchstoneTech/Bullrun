Bull Run: Market Runner

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026 · Last updated 6 September 2026

The short version

Bull Run has no account, no sign-up and no server. Your coins, your records, your roster and your settings live on your phone and nowhere else. There is no analytics SDK and no crash reporter in the app.

The one thing that can leave your device is your advertising identifier, and only if you tap Allow when iOS asks — it is used to serve the adverts you choose to watch. Say no and every reward in the game pays exactly the same.

What stays on your device

Everything the game knows about you is written to your own phone's app storage and is never uploaded to us:

  • Your portfolio records, best runs, sets, and per-mode high scores.
  • Coins, gold, the characters and trails you own, and which are equipped.
  • Daily challenges, streaks, medals, and your last twenty runs' earnings.
  • Your settings — sound, music, haptics, reduce motion, and the 9pm reminder.
  • One display preference for the Book's chart, stored in iOS's standard app preferences.

We cannot see any of it. Deleting the app deletes all of it, and Settings → Reset progress inside the game erases it without uninstalling.

Advertising

Bull Run shows rewarded adverts only — every advert in the game is one you chose to watch in exchange for something, and there is no interstitial anywhere in the app. Adverts are served by Unity Ads, a service of Unity Technologies.

Tracking, and your answer to it

After your first run the game asks, through Apple's own prompt, whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared with Unity so the adverts you see can be personalised, which is worth more to the game.

Saying no changes nothing about what you are paid. Every door in the game — the double, the bell, the mint, the vault draw — pays the same amount either way. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Install measurement

The app supports SKAdNetwork, Apple's privacy-preserving attribution system, which tells an advertiser that an install happened without identifying you. It works whether or not you allow tracking and is handled entirely by iOS.

What Unity receives

To serve an advert, Unity may receive your advertising identifier (only with your permission), device and OS information, coarse location inferred from your IP address, and information about the adverts you were shown. Unity's handling of that data is governed by their own policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple's Game Center, and it is entirely optional — declining costs you nothing but the online boards. If you are signed in, your best scores and earned achievements are sent to Apple under your own Game Center account, and other players may see your Game Center nickname next to your rank.

The game never sees your Apple ID or your email address. You can sign out in iOS Settings → Game Center. If you never sign in, the boards in the game show generated stand-in names instead, and the app says so on the screen.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules one local reminder a day at 9pm in your own time zone. It is created and delivered entirely by your phone — there is no push server, we send nothing, and we are not told whether you opened it. Turn it off with The bell in the game's settings, or in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Any in-app purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details — the app is told only whether a purchase exists, so it can unlock what you paid for.

What we never collect

To be specific rather than reassuring, Bull Run does not collect, ask for, or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account credential.
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar or health data.
  • Precise location. The app never requests location permission.
  • Any behavioural analytics, session recording or crash telemetry.

Children

Bull Run is not directed at children under 13, and the app is not configured as a child-directed service with its advertising provider. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will act on it — though in practice there is nothing held on our side to remove.

Your choices

  • Stop tracking — iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Stop the reminder — the game's Settings, or iOS Settings → Notifications.
  • Leave the boards — sign out in iOS Settings → Game Center.
  • Erase your progress — Settings → Reset progress in the game.
  • Erase everything — delete the app. Nothing survives on our side, because nothing was ever there.

Because the game holds no personal data on any server, there is no account for us to export or delete on request. Requests about data held by Unity or Apple should go to them under the policies linked on this page.

Third parties

  • Unity Technologies — advertising
  • Apple — App Store, Game Center and SKAdNetwork
